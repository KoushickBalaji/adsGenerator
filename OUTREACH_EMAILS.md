# Outreach Email Drafts (India Jewellery Brands)

These are short, low-friction outreach drafts designed for small brands where the only published inbox might be `info@` / `care@`.

## Email 1 (First Touch)

**Subject options**
- Quick idea for {Brand}: product videos from a URL
- Can I send 3 sample videos for {Brand}?
- 15 mins? I built an auto ad-video generator for jewellery

**Body**
Hi {Name/Team},

I’m building a tool that generates short product video ads from a single product URL (it uses your existing product photos and page details — no shoot needed).

If you’re open to it, I can generate **3 sample videos for 3 of your SKUs** this week. You only need to share the product links — no access required.

If this sounds relevant, could you reply with **3 product URLs** (or tell me who handles marketing/partnerships and I’ll forward)?

Thanks,  
{Your Name}  
{Your City (Bangalore)} · {WhatsApp/Phone}  
{Demo link (optional)}

## Email 2 (Follow-up, Day 2)

**Subject**
Re: product videos for {Brand}

**Body**
Hi {Name/Team} — quick bump.

Happy to generate 3 sample videos for {Brand} if you share 3 product links. If you’re not the right contact, could you please connect me to the person who handles growth/marketing?

Thanks,  
{Your Name}

## Email 3 (Follow-up with Proof, Day 5)

**Subject**
Sample video for {Brand} (from your product page)

**Body**
Hi {Name/Team},

I generated a sample video from one of your product pages so you can see the output:
{Sample Link}

If you’d like, I can do 2 more samples for 2 more SKUs — just send the links.

Thanks,  
{Your Name}
